# The Lighthouse in the Fog

A boat must reach the harbor. The north beacon says the route is clear, a
sailor reports an obstacle, and the south beacon is silent.

1. **Receive** — Omega hears the conflicting reports through a real channel.
2. **Remember** — Omega recalls yesterday's driftwood report.
3. **Verify** — Omega reads a current, identified harbor bulletin.
4. **Reason** — Omega compares source quality, missing information, and
   conflicting evidence with MeTTa/NAL/STV.
5. **Explain** — Omega recommends a route for human review, states what remains
   unknown, and leaves a receipt.

Omega does not steer the boat. A person decides.

## The one-line model

> Omega receives, remembers, verifies, reasons, and explains. A person decides.

## What the lesson proves

The lesson is designed to prove one observable OmegaClaw path: continuous loop,
channel input/output, bounded memory, one controlled file read, MeTTa/NAL/STV,
provenance, a revised conclusion, and a receipt.

It does not prove every provider, channel, skill, PLN/ONA behavior, web search,
shell access, self-improvement, or safe production autonomy.
